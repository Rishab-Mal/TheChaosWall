Why this matters

A double pendulum is a conservative system — its total mechanical energy E = T + V cannot change. Any model that violates this will eventually produce unphysical trajectories. The ground truth integrator uses a symplectic method that preserves E exactly. The HNN learns the scalar Hamiltonian H(q, p) and derives dynamics via dq/dt = ∂H/∂p, dp/dt = −∂H/∂q — this structure guarantees that H (and thus E) is conserved by construction.

The LSTM has no such constraint. It minimises prediction error step-by-step with no knowledge of conservation laws. Each step introduces a tiny energy error; over hundreds of steps these compound, and the trajectory drifts to arbitrarily high or low energy states — physically impossible, and increasingly wrong. Smaller ε in the Butterfly page, longer duration here — together they show exactly why naïve sequence models fail at physical simulation.
